# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Mi Casa Su Casa to be a welcoming, respectful project for everyone who participates.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intent
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person
- Help keep discussions practical and kind

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Repeated hostility or bad-faith disruption
- Publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or block participation when needed to protect the project community.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
